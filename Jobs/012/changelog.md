# Changelog — Job #012

*The game has a place to stand. Until now you spawned onto an empty baseplate; the Workshop existed
only as a paragraph in the design.*

- 🏭 **The Workshop exists.** A 104 × 104 stud factory hall with hazard-striped edging, grated
  walkways, pillars and machinery, built from the industrial kit.
- 🪧 **All seven stations are named in neon** — MAGNET LAB, RECYCLER, REPAIR, ROBOT BAY,
  PART ARCHIVE, FACTORY ENTRANCE and SCRAP ARENA — each glowing in its own colour, so you can tell
  what a station does before you read the word.
- 👁️ **You can see out to where the Arena will be.** There is a real opening in the east wall, and
  the game now *checks* that nothing is standing in front of it. (It caught something on the first
  try: the Scrap Arena's own sign was blocking the view of the Arena.)
- 🚶 **You spawn in the hall**, facing the opening.

**Not in yet, on purpose:** none of the stations *do* anything — walking up to the Recycler will not
recycle. That is the next job. The Arena beyond the opening does not exist yet either; the opening
looks out at empty space until group 10.
