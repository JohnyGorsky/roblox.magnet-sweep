## 🔴 A claim this job published, and then retracted

**I reported that `BasePart.Reflectance` is "effectively inert here". That was wrong.**

The independent reviewer flagged it as unsupported, citing Roblox's own docs: *"may or may not be
ignored depending on the `Material` of the part."* Re-measured with a positive control and a non-metal:

| Material | Reflectance 0.0 → 1.0 |
|---|---|
| `Metal` | no visible change — inert |
| `SmoothPlastic` | **clear progression** — pale diffuse → dark, strongly reflective |

The original test used **only Metal** and generalised to the whole place. Corrected in the style skill,
`Config/Quality`, `systems/vfx-lighting` and decision 0016.

The practical conclusion survives — the Low tier drops the variant rather than falling back to
Reflectance — but for the right reason now: Reflectance is inert on `Metal`, which is the base material
of **five of the nine** kit surfaces.

## 🔴 The methodological error underneath it

Every material judgement in the first half of this job was made under **Studio default lighting** —
flat grey ambient at 0.275, Brightness 3, no ColorCorrection, Atmosphere haze and glare at 0 — not the
style skill's recipe. Flat bright ambient washes out specular contrast, which is exactly the condition
that makes a mirror look dull and a textured surface look acceptable.

`docs/build/job-order.md`'s first sequencing rule says precisely this: *"every visual judgement made
before job 006 is made under the wrong light."* The rule was written in this repo and then broken in
the next job.

Re-tested under the correct recipe. The chrome conclusion held, but the *reasoning* did not:

> **"A roughness map cannot make a mirror" is false.** Roughness ≈ 0 with metalness = 1 **is** a mirror
> in any PBR renderer. What defeats the generated chrome is that the generator also emits a textured
> ColorMap and NormalMap, which break the reflection up.

And `job-order.md` listed *"the 18 flat greyscale metalness/roughness maps"* as a deliverable of this
job. They were **never authored** — generated maps were used instead. So hand-authored flats remain the
untested route to a true PBR mirror, and the style skill's Metalness/Roughness columns are **authoring
intent, not shipped values**.

## Generator findings, recorded for job 005

- **Long prompts fail** (`MaterialGenTool:650: invalid argument #2 to 'assert'`); shortening the same
  prompt succeeds. Four of nine needed a retry.
- **The word "hazard" trips a filter.** Two failures until rephrased purely descriptively.
- **Short prompts succeed but produce bland output.** A real tradeoff, not a free win.
- Generation yields **4 candidates**; promote the pick to a **direct child** of `MaterialService`.

## Known issues, not fixed

- **`MS_PaintedGloss` cannot take a zone accent** — its ColorMap has red baked in, and Color multiplies.
  Regenerate neutral when zone colour work starts.
- **`MS_Rubber` has no belt ribbing** — comes from geometry + texture offset instead.
- **Neither Reflectance measurement has been confirmed in Play.** Both were Edit. Edit is defensible for
  material appearance since the same renderer draws it, but PITFALLS #1 still applies.
