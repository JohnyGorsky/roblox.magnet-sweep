# Changelog — Job #007

*The first thing in MAGNET SWEEP you can actually play.*

- 🧲 **The magnet works.** Walk into a field of scrap: nearby pieces shake and spark, pieces in
  range lift off the floor and fly to your magnet, and they bank as you collect them.
- 🔩 **Things too heavy to lift tell you so** — they shake harder and never break free, so the
  reason to upgrade Magnet Power is something you see rather than read.
- 📱 **Your phone gets a phone-sized budget.** How many objects can be in flight at once is now set
  by the device you are actually on, not by one number chosen for everybody.
- ⚡ **Collection is honest and it is fast.** Every collected object is checked against what the
  server really spawned and where it really was; in normal play nothing you collect is ever
  refused, and if something is, it comes back into view instead of vanishing.
- 🧹 **Scrap you abandon comes home.** Wander off mid-pull, or die, or alt-tab, and the objects you
  left in the air return to where they were instead of falling out of the world — and your magnet
  keeps working.
