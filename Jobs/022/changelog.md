# Changelog — job 022

🏭 **The Workshop is yours now.** Join the game and a bay is assigned to you automatically — your
face and name light up on the wall above it, and the bay wakes up with them.

🤖 **Your robot is standing in it.** Everyone starts with the same five rusty stubs — a barely-working
work lamp for a head, a seized motor, a dented paint drum, a bent pipe wrench and squeaky caster
wheels. Nothing you ever find is worse than them.

🔧 **You can rebuild it.** Walk up to the ROBOT BAY, press BUILD, and pick a slot: head, core, body,
arm, back or wheels. Every part you own is listed with what it does, and the one currently bolted on
says so. Choose another and it is on the robot before you close the panel.

💾 **And it stays.** The game now remembers you — your robot comes back exactly as you left it the
next time you play.

🥄 **24 parts can be built** so far, from the Color Workshop, Toy Assembly and Mega Kitchen — including
the Giant Spoon, the Toy Hammer and the Refrigerator Door.

✨ Text on every button in the game is sharper.
