# Changelog — Job #011

*The game finally shows you what you are doing. Until now the magnet worked and nothing on screen
said so — your coins, your scrap and your combo were only visible by typing a dev command.*

- 🪙 **You can see your Coins**, top left, counting up as you sweep.
- 🧲 **Your scrap and how much more will fit** sit at the bottom — and turn red when the magnet is
  full, so the thing you already felt (everything stops lifting) now has a number next to it.
- ⚡ **MAGNET FLOW appears while a combo is running** and disappears when it is not, so it means
  something when it is on screen. It climbs ×1 → ×2 → ×3 → ×4 and then becomes **MAGNET RUSH!**
- 📢 **Short banners** call out the moments in one line: MAGNET RUSH!, SCRAP FULL, OVERCHARGE.
- 🛒 **Coins now buy something.** An upgrade button opens the Magnet Lab: Magnet Power, Pull Radius,
  Capacity and Magnetic Drive, each with what it is now, what it becomes, and what it costs.
- 📱 **Laid out for a phone first, and measured on one.** Nothing sits under your thumbs — the game
  reads where Roblox has put its own move and jump controls and stays out of both. Opening the
  Magnet Lab hides those controls so the panel has the screen to itself.
- 🔢 **Numbers count up and give a small kick** when they change, instead of snapping.

**Not in yet, on purpose:** the robot and Arena readout is built and wired, but stays hidden until
there is a real robot to report — it will not show you invented numbers. Buttons are silent until a
human picks a click sound; the game lists that empty slot at every startup rather than shipping a
guess.
