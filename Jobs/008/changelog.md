# Changelog — Job #008

- ⚡ **Magnet Flow.** Keep collecting and your magnet builds a combo — better coin payouts and a
  faster pull. Stop, and it drains away.
- 🌟 **MAGNET RUSH.** Fill the combo and the magnet goes wide for eight seconds: a much bigger
  field and far more in the air at once. Then it is spent, and you have to earn it again.
- 📦 **SCRAP FULL.** Your magnet holds only so much. When it is full it visibly stops lifting —
  everything still rattles in range — and it is time to go and recycle.
- 🏃 **Magnetic Drive** now actually moves you: upgrading it makes you faster, and it stays with
  you through a respawn. Carrying something heavy slows you down.
- 🔧 Power, Radius, Drive and Capacity are all real upgradeable stats now instead of fixed
  starter numbers.
