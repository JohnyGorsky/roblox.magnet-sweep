# Endgame

Everything after zone 12. Sections 76-82.

## The first ending — The Foundry Heart (section 76)

After zone 12 the player enters **THE FOUNDRY HEART**: a huge final magnet mechanism. They pull its core
free. The factory goes dark. Lights restart. A robot arrives. Short cinematic.

```
        YOU REACHED THE CORE
                 ↓
    THE ENDLESS LINE HAS OPENED
```

This is not the final ending. It unlocks the endgame.

## The Endless Line (section 77)

A procedural / semi-procedural corridor assembled from **curated room modules** — not uncontrolled
runtime generation. Every segment increases mass, object size, reward, security and rare-part quality.

The metric is **DISTANCE**:

```
12,840m
```

with a global leaderboard.

> Curated modules, not generation. A procedurally generated factory produces rooms nobody designed, and
> this game's rooms are the reason to walk through them. Same reasoning as Tide's curated islands.

## Relic Parts (section 78)

The Endless Line introduces its own class of part, much harder to recover:

Black Hole Core · Planetary Drill · Reactor Crown · Gravity Claw · Titan Plate · Plasma Wings ·
Magnetic Halo

*(Slots, rarities and effects are unspecified in the source. They need defining before the Endless Line
ships.)*

## Overclock (section 79)

[Decision 0013](../decisions/0013-overclock-not-rebirth.md). Not a rebirth.

**Resets:** zone access, Coins, magnet upgrade levels, Drive upgrades. *(Four items — Capacity is
not among them.)*
**Keeps:** robot parts, the collection archive, cosmetics, Arena stats, the robot's name.
**Awards:** a permanent **MAGNET CORE LEVEL** — starting Magnet Power, plus a percentage discount on
every zone gate. The curve bends; the gates stay. See
[decision 0013](../decisions/0013-overclock-not-rebirth.md#how-magnet-core-level-bends-the-curve).

Subsequent runs are faster and more chaotic.

## Six endgame goals (section 80)

The endgame is deliberately plural. Different players chase different boards:

| Goal | Metric |
|---|---|
| **Explorer** | furthest Endless Line distance |
| **Collector** | complete all robot parts |
| **Builder** | strongest or most unusual robot |
| **Arena Player** | longest Arena control |
| **Fighter** | most robots defeated |
| **Completionist** | complete the Factory Archive |

## Daily leaderboards (section 81)

**LONGEST ARENA HOLD** · **FURTHEST DISTANCE** · **ROBOTS DEFEATED** · **RARE PARTS RECOVERED** ·
**LARGEST OBJECT PULLED**

Five boards, five playstyles. "Largest object pulled" is the fun one — it rewards the exact behaviour
the game is named after.

## Live content (section 82)

Daily Factory Modifier · Weekly Legendary Part · Weekend Arena Modifier · limited cosmetics.

> **Never make a limited gameplay part permanently superior.** Limited *cosmetics* are the lever.
