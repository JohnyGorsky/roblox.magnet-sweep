# FINDING 0009: CFrame.fromMatrix with a left-handed basis mirrored 1701 parts and rendered every mesh inside-out

**Project:** `roblox.magnet-sweep`
**Status:** open
**Severity:** high
**Created:** 2026-09-06 10:57:21

**Symptom:** SYMPTOM as reported by the owner: mesh seems invisible, i can see through, and later all textures are fucked up in game area, in demo area they are good. Both were the same bug and the first report was misdiagnosed as the camera being inside the mesh. CAUSE: CFrame.fromMatrix(pos, vX, vY, vZ) requires vX cross vY == vZ. Job 020 placed every wall panel, building, base, divider and name plate with fromMatrix(pos, tang, Vector3.yAxis, n) where tang = (-sin a, 0, cos a) and n = (cos a, 0, sin a). But tang cross up = -n, NOT +n, so every one of those calls built a LEFT-HANDED frame with determinant -1. A mirrored transform flips triangle winding, so the renderer backface-culls the OUTSIDE of a mesh and draws its INSIDE: the Part Archive showed a pale flat interior instead of its dark lattice with cyan pockets and hazard stripe. MEASURED: 1701 of 2069 BaseParts in workspace.Hub had det -1 (Wall 1120/1172, Bases 505/543, Dividers 33/33, Signage 16/16, Fixtures 27/29). Floor tiles were 0/276 because they were placed with .Position, not fromMatrix. Arena 0/692 and DemoRoom 0/933 - both built by other code. Plain bricks look fine when mirrored, which is why this survived four rounds of review and several eye-level screenshots; only meshes and textured faces show it. NOT caused by the x0.5 world rescale, which preserved rotation exactly - the bug was present from the moment the room was built. FIX APPLIED: for each mirrored part, CFrame.fromMatrix(cf.Position, -cf.RightVector, cf.UpVector), which rebuilds a right-handed frame keeping position and LookVector identical; 242 child Attachment WorldCFrames were recorded and reassigned because mirroring the parent frame moves attachments stored in local space. Hub is now 0/2069 mirrored. PREVENTION: never pass a third basis vector to fromMatrix unless you have verified the cross product; prefer the 3-argument form fromMatrix(pos, vX, vY) which derives vZ correctly, or assert that the determinant is +1 after building any frame. WRONG THEORIES BURNED FIRST, all disproved by measurement: mesh LOD/RenderFidelity (set Precise on 170 meshes, no change, reverted to Automatic), physical size (restored the mesh to 40 studs, still broken), orientation/back-face (dot(look,inward)=1.00 on both), material properties (identical TextureID/Material/Color/MaterialVariant, no SurfaceAppearance on either), lighting (flattened Brightness/Ambient/environment scales/shadows/Atmosphere/post - difference unchanged), occlusion, texture streaming and quality level.
