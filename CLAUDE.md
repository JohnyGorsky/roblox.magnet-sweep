## The one-line version

**MAGNET SWEEP** is a physics collection + extraction + robot-building game. Pull scrap with a magnet,
go deeper into the factory, rip out a ridiculous Robot Part, escape security with it, bolt it onto your
homemade robot, and release that robot into a persistent Arena. Mobile-first. **One place.**
