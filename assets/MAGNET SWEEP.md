# MAGNET SWEEP
## Master Game Intake - A to Z

**Working title:** MAGNET SWEEP  
**Tagline:** Find it. Pull it. Bring it home. Bolt it on.  
**Secondary tagline:** Build the craziest robot in the factory.

---

# 1. High-Level Concept

MAGNET SWEEP is a colorful physics-driven Roblox game combining:

- satisfying magnet collection
- ASMR-style sound and VFX
- forward progression through increasingly dangerous factory zones
- rare physical robot-part hunting
- extraction / escape gameplay
- player-built robots
- persistent automated robot arena combat
- arena territory control
- strategic resource allocation
- endless progression

The player begins with a tiny magnet capable of pulling screws, nuts and washers.

As the magnet improves, the player travels deeper into an enormous continuously progressing factory.

Objects become:

- larger
- heavier
- rarer
- more valuable
- more dangerous to retrieve

Some objects are not simply scrap.

They are **Robot Parts**.

A giant spoon can become an arm.

A STOP sign can become a shield.

A refrigerator door can become armor.

A motorcycle wheel can become robot mobility.

An excavator bucket can become a devastating weapon.

Players physically retrieve these objects and build a unique robot from whatever they discover.

That robot can then be released into a persistent server arena where it automatically fights other players' robots for control of the central Arena Core.

The robot remains damaged after combat and requires scrap to repair.

This creates the primary economic decision:

> Do I use this scrap to improve myself or keep my robot fighting?

# 2. Primary Player Fantasy

The player should feel:

> "I started by collecting screws. Now I'm running away from a security robot while dragging an engine behind me so I can put it inside my homemade fighting robot."

Eventually:

> "My robot has an excavator arm, satellite head, car-door armor and a nuclear-looking power core, and it has controlled the arena for eight minutes."

Players should visually see progression through:

Controls:

- maximum object weight
- ability to detach rare parts
- gate requirements

---

## 🌀 Magnet Radius

Controls:

how far objects begin being pulled.

Question:

> HOW MUCH can I sweep?

---

## ⚡ Magnetic Drive

Controls movement speed.

Question:

> CAN I ESCAPE?

Base:

approximately 16 studs/sec.

Late progression:

approximately 30-36 before endgame modifiers.

Controls ordinary scrap carrying capacity.

# 23. Guardians

Each zone has a unique threat.

If caught:

player is knocked down.

Rare part drops.

Player gets approximately:

**5 seconds**

to recover it.

Otherwise security reclaims it.

Player does NOT lose:

- Coins
- magnet progression
- secured parts

---

# 24. Player Competition

Players cannot casually steal secured cargo from each other.

Rare server parts can become competitive.

Once detached:

player gets short ownership protection.

If guardian knocks carrier down and player fails to recover:

part becomes neutral.

Another player may attempt extraction.

This creates tension without constant griefing.
